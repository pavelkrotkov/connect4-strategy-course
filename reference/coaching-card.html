The Coaching Card

Stage-based, not age-based: read what your kid reliably does, play one notch above it, and let them discover the rest. From Lesson 7.

The ladder

Stage 0 — drops anywhere
Signs: no reaction to your threats. You play: loud verticals only — towers are visible and always blockable (L3). Takebacks allowed. Graduates when: they block your tower without prompting.
Stage 1 — blocks completed threes
Signs: instant blocks of threes, blind to pairs. You play: slow horizontal pairs; let an open three happen to you and lose to it honestly. Name their blocks specifically. Graduates when: they block a pair before it becomes a three — or make their first double threat.
Stage 2 — kills pairs, attacks openly
Signs: preemptive blocks, loud attacks. You play: quiet shapes — build a 7 and leave it unsprung; open at c/e; bank only even claims (L3). One question per game, asked at the brink: "look up — what lands on top of that?" Graduates when: they refuse to touch a poisoned column.
Stage 3 — builds structure
Signs: their own double threats and stacks. You play: honestly, minus one scan question (skip Q4 — never kill their structure early). Post-game: replay exactly one moment. Graduates when: they beat you playing this way, twice.
Stage 4 — runs the scan
You play: for real, alternating who goes first. Autopsy surprising games together on the solver. You've worked yourself out of the coaching job — this is the goal.

The four scripts

One question per game. Asked at the moment of decision, never after: "what am I threatening right now?" — then silence. Where it's been measured, guided beats told — and both beat unguided. (guided-play meta-analysis)

Look up before you drop. The gift-check (L6 Q3) as a question, reserved for the moment their hand hovers over a poisoned column.

Replay one moment. Post-game, rebuild a single position — "what else could you have played here?" — never the whole game, never a lecture.

Praise the strategy, not the smarts. "You saw that trap two moves early" beats "you're so good at this."

The handicap menu (visible → invisible)

① Takebacks (stage 0 only) · ② open at c/e — an honest draw from move one (L5) · ③ bank only even claims — you'll threaten loudly and never cash (L3) · ④ skip scan Q4 — beatable by anyone who builds (L6) · ⑤ endgame kill-switch — burn a tempo in your own claim's column (L4).

The one rule: never fake a blunder. Kids sense thrown games, and a fake win teaches nothing. Every lever above is an honest strategy played fully — you can genuinely lose from these stances, which is the point. (every chess parent ever; for the general craft of teaching kids a game, ChessKid)
